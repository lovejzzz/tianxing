## English

### What's new — 1.5

#### Loading Bay

A new game in Rush. Fill the trailer before he drives out. A parcel only turns clockwise, and the last one in is the first one out, so load for the order of his route: straight on when he can, right when he can't. Every day there is more to carry, and now and then a house gives him a coin. Opens at 54 stars.
