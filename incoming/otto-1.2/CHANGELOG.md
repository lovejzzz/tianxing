## English

### What's new — 1.2

#### Tin Pallet

A new way to play in Rush. A steel board on a tray, held in two hands; the wooden tiles slide when you tip it, the steel and the glass stay put. Make him a road, press the green button, and he goes. Tilt the phone, or use the stick. Your best time is kept. Opens at 108 stars.

*Picture: `changelog/tinpallet.jpg`*

#### Rampage, wilder

A red portal and a blue one: in at one, out of the other, still going the same way. After forty seconds, a duck with a bomb on its back and ten seconds on the clock: push it into the water and the ground around it is patched, the crates there are yours; leave it, and it takes the floor with it. And you can ride any duck now, the mother too. It runs twice as fast, and goes red, and smokes.

*Picture: `changelog/rampage2.jpg`*

#### How to play

Now at the top of Options: two small boards to lay and send him down yourself, with a finger showing where. The boards also come back after every 8:00, and SKIP asks before it goes.

*Picture: `changelog/howto.jpg`*

#### New headphones

The headphones before the title are made again. The old pair (left) has made way for a 1970s hi-fi pair: stitched leather, brushed aluminium, a coiled cord, an amber lamp that breathes. Finish the first night, and the next time you open the game Otto's own pair is waiting (right).

*Picture: `changelog/headphones.jpg`*

### And

- A shell you earn now shows itself: its poster comes up and catches the light. Touch it to see its page; go back, and you play on where you were.
- Night Mail and Night Cab are now 5 coins each, or both together as Super Late.
- SEND HIM goes grey when the road is laid the same way it already failed. You can still press it.
- A plank you knock out breaks and sinks. Put it back, and the same pieces come up out of the water.
- On every road of the first district, the line showing where he will go turns green once it gets him home. The district's last road is new, and harder.
- At 8:00 the finger is always there, and draws back slowly when you send him.
- Tap your coins in the store to see where coins come from; your coins and what they went on are now shown as pictures, with how each shell was earned.
- Every question now dims the screen, softens it, and puts the sound under water.
- Open a shell from this page and go back: you land here again, where you were.
- And one shell that only comes to those who keep trying.
- A new icon, printed in four inks.

## 简体中文

### 更新内容 — 1.2

#### Tin Pallet

Rush 里的新玩法。一双手托着一块钢板。倾斜它，木砖会滑动，钢砖和玻璃砖不动。给他拼出一条路，按下绿色按钮，他就出发。可以倾斜手机，也可以用摇杆。会记下你最快的时间。拥有 108 颗星即可开放。

*Picture: `changelog/tinpallet.jpg`*

#### 暴走，更乱了

一红一蓝两扇传送门：从一扇进去，从另一扇出来，方向不变。四十秒后会来一只背着炸弹的鸭子，身上倒数十秒：把它推进水里，周围的地会补好，那里的箱子都归你；不管它，它就把那一片地一起炸掉。现在还能骑上任何一只鸭子，包括鸭妈妈。骑上去它跑得快一倍，会变红，会冒烟。

*Picture: `changelog/rampage2.jpg`*

#### 怎么玩

现在在选项的最上面：两块小地图，自己铺砖、自己送他出发，手指会告诉你点哪里。每次打完 8:00 也会再出现，点 SKIP 之前会先问你一句。

*Picture: `changelog/howto.jpg`*

#### 新耳机

标题前的那副耳机重做了。旧的那副（左）让位给一副 70 年代的 Hi-Fi 耳机：缝线皮头梁、拉丝铝耳罩、弹簧线，还有一颗会呼吸的琥珀灯。打通第一夜之后，下次打开游戏，等着你的是 Otto 自己的那副（右）。

*Picture: `changelog/headphones.jpg`*

### 还有

- 得到新外壳时，它的海报会弹出来，闪一下光。点一下能看它的介绍；返回就接着玩刚才那一关。
- 夜邮和午夜出租现在各 5 金币，也可以买套装「超级晚」一起带走。
- 路面和上次没走通时一模一样，SEND HIM 会变灰，但照样可以按。
- 敲掉的木板会碎开、沉下去。放回去时，同样的碎片会从水里浮回来。
- 第一个街区的每一关，预判路线能把他送到终点时会变绿。这个街区的最后一关是新的，也更难了。
- 8:00 那一关的手指一直都在，你送他出发时它会慢慢缩回去。
- 在商店点右上角的金币，可以看到金币从哪里来；金币和你换来的外壳现在都用图来显示，靠条件拿到的外壳会写着怎么拿到的。
- 每次弹出询问，画面都会暗下来、变模糊，声音也像沉到水里。
- 从这一页点开外壳再返回，会回到这一页原来的位置。
- 还有一件外壳，只给一直不放弃的人。
- 新图标，像四色印刷的版画。
