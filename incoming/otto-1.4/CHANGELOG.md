## English

### What's new — 1.4

#### Glass Town

A new game in Rush. A district of cracked glass, and three tries a board. Pick any tile on the edge and he sets off from it; the glass he drives off stays broken for the tries after, so a try that fails can break the glass the next one needs. Reach the flag and the next board comes. The newsroom behind the glass puts it on the air. Opens at 180 stars.

### 更新内容 — 1.4

#### 碎玻璃城

Rush 里的新玩法。一片全是裂玻璃的街区，每关三次机会。点边上任意一块砖，他就从那里出发；他开过的玻璃会碎，而且之后几次都碎着，所以没成功的那一次，可以替下一次把需要的玻璃先踩碎。到了旗子就进下一关。玻璃后面的新闻导播室会把这一切播出去。拥有 180 颗星即可开放。
