## What the site must not name

Nothing in 1.4 is secret. The store already shows the Christmas Gifts set and the names of its three liveries, locked, before Christmas Eve, so all of them can be named.

One thing to get right: Christmas Gifts is not sold. It is given. Anyone who opens the game on Christmas Eve 2026 or later gets the set. Don't give it a price or say it can be bought.
