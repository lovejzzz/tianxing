## English

### What's new — 1.3

#### Rival

A new game in Rush, against 0771. One board, one bag of tiles, a turn each: lay road, glass and arrow stickers, send your robot, and take the parcel before it does. You can see where the next one will land, so plan for two at once, or wait on the spot and let it drop on you. In the last round every parcel is a present, worth two. 0771 thinks out loud while it plays. One coin to sit down; win, and you take two; draw, and you have yours back. Opens at 144 stars.

### And

- New sounds all through the game, and through the first night's story: the cuts between scenes, the claw that brings him back, the lights coming on across the map.
- Rampage: the arena is bigger, and your coins are at the top right; each one you win flies up into them.
- Rampage: when you ask to leave, the game stands still behind the question.
- Tin Pallet: the tilt sign on the panel is bigger, and easier to touch.
- How to play: SKIP comes up when you tap the screen three times quickly.
- Quieter where it should be: the headphones before the title come up in silence, a new road's name no longer chimes, and the bells, the title cards' tone, the rain on his shell and a few small whooshes are gone.
- The store: each week two single shells and one from a set are on sale, and the sets stand cheapest first.
- Super Late has a new poster, two windows one over the other. Night Mail and Night Cab are now 15 coins each, or 21 together.

## 简体中文

### 更新内容 — 1.3

#### Rival

Rush 里的新玩法：和 0771 对战。同一块棋盘、同一袋砖，轮流出手：铺路砖、玻璃砖、贴箭头贴纸，送你的机器人出发，赶在它前面拿到包裹。下一个包裹会落在哪里提前看得到，可以算好一次拿两个，也可以站在那里等它砸到你头上。最后一轮的包裹都包成礼物，每个算 2 分。0771 一边下一边把想法说出来。入场 1 金币，赢了拿走 2 枚，平局退回你的 1 枚。拥有 144 颗星即可开放。

### 还有

- 整个游戏和第一夜的剧情都多了声音：场景之间的黑场、把他吊回起点的机械爪、地图上街区的灯亮起来。
- 暴走：场地更大了；你的金币显示在右上角，每赢一枚都会飞进去。
- 暴走：点「离开」时，游戏会停在问题后面，等你回答。
- Tin Pallet：面板上的倾斜标志更大，也更好点。
- 怎么玩：快速点三下屏幕，SKIP 才会出现。
- 该安静的地方安静了：标题前的耳机安静地出现，进入新一关时地名不再「叮」一声；钟声、时段标题卡的声音、雨打在机身上的声音和几处轻微的风声都去掉了。
- 商店：每周有两件单卖的外壳和一件套装里的外壳打折；套装从便宜到贵排列。
- Super Late 换了新海报：两个叠在一起的窗口。夜邮和午夜出租现在各 15 金币，套装一起 21。
